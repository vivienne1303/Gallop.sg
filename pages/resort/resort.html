Horse And Wellness Escape

Enjoy a rustic overnight camp designed for riders, families, and nature seekers looking to slow down and reconnect with horses. Nestled away from the city's bustle, our cozy container-style cabins offer a simple, restful stay surrounded by gentle grazing horses and open green spaces. A horse wellness camp where guests can experience the rhythm of stable life, feed the horses, clean the stables, learn how to care for them, and join guided rides or horse activities. Whether you're a first-time rider or simply longing for a quiet escape, Gallop Resort invites you to breathe and discover the simple joy of living alongside horses, a soft return to nature's pace.
