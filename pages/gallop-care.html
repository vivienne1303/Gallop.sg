Gallop Cares

Galloping towards inclusion

Gentle equine-assisted experiences for special needs, elderly groups and differently-abled communities.

Where horses meet every heart

Inclusion without exception

Gallop Cares supports calm, confidence and emotional regulation through stillness, attunement and the quiet bond between people and horses.

  • Gentle equine moments for special needs and eldercare groups
  • Workshops designed to nurture everyday skills and self-expression
  • Safe, respectful experiences for differently-abled communities
  • Rescued horses and ponies as partners in confidence building

Rescue care

Gallop Stable began with 13 ponies and has grown into a home for over 160 rescued racehorses.

Accessible riding

With no membership fees, Gallop Stable keeps horse riding approachable and meaningful for more people.

Gallop Green

Grazing for a greener tomorrow

Gallop Stable's sustainability promise includes solarized power, recycling waste, rainwater harvesting and turning horse waste into nutrient-rich organic fertilizer.
