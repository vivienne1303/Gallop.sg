Jackuda Activities

A family haven of horsey activities

Pony rides, trail rides, bunny feeding, art corners, and workshops delight wide-eyed kids and grown-ups, too. Host your child's birthday, wedding, or next corporate team building in our cosy function rooms or take the magic outdoors with our pony and horse rentals for bespoke parties, sunset shoots, pre-wedding shoots, or community events. From stable tours to hands-on pony grooming sessions and guided trail rides through nature, Gallop Jackuda brings families, friends, and colleagues together to bond and celebrate in the gentle company of horses.
