Flavours Of Yesterday

Gallop Catering brings people together through Singapore's nostalgic food traditions, heritage flavours, local delights, and the age-old art of sharing meals the kampung way. Our rustic food truck stalls serve local favourites and comforting bites that invite everyone to slow down, gather, and rediscover the forgotten ways of coming together over food. Whether you're here for a hearty meal after a trail ride, planning a birthday, or hosting a community gathering by the paddocks, Gallop Catering keeps the kampung spirit alive. Celebrating Singapore's roots through food, connection, and the simple joy of sharing.
