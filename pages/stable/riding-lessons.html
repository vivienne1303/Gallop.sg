Activities for every age and experience level

Gallop Stable offers a full range of riding activities from lessons to joy rides and trail rides for all ages; tiny tots to adults. We offer our riders a safe, supportive and encouraging environment to explore and further their passion in all aspects of horsemanship and riding.

Pony Trail Ride

3 years and above

We encourage children to start riding at a young age. For very young riders, this casual private session is a fun and safe introduction to ponies.

Beginners

4 years and above

For beginner riders, we recommend a private or group lesson covering the basic principles of riding, including leading your horse, mounting, correct riding position, walking, trotting, stopping and safely dismounting. Riders who begin with private lessons can move into a suitable group lesson once their basic riding skills are established.

Riding is a physically demanding sport. Young and beginner riders may not yet have the strength, stamina and concentration required for longer lessons, so we recommend starting with shorter sessions and progressing gradually. Building confidence takes time, and it is important not to rush through the foundational levels.

Novice

For riders with some experience who want to further develop their skills, we offer private and group lessons. Novice riders should have a strong foundation in basic riding skills and feel secure and confident in the saddle.

These lessons focus on improving riding technique, developing the seat and hands, learning to canter and riding over poles.

We also recommend group lessons so riders can become confident around other horses and riders. This is an essential skill for competitions, events and trail rides, while also giving riders opportunities to learn from one another and build supportive friendships.

Advanced

Gallop also offers lessons for experienced riders. Sessions include lateral movements, advanced schooling exercises and a greater focus on riding the horse correctly and effectively.

Lessons may include show jumping. Riders are encouraged to speak with their instructors about any particular areas, subjects or skills they would like to work on.
