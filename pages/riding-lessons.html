Important notes

Before enrolment

Registration: A one-time registration fee of S$30 applies for all new enrolments.
Attire: Proper riding attire is compulsory for all riders, including the Gallop T-shirt at S$30.
Rental: Helmet rental is S$5 and boots rental is S$5.
Starter pack: Includes school uniform, breeches, boots and helmet.
