Education beyond the classroom

A gentle and engaging stable experience

Bring learning to life with an exciting and interactive horse experience at Gallop Stable. Our Learning Journey Programme gives students the opportunity to discover the world of horses and ponies through guided stable tours, hands-on grooming sessions, pony feeding, and a supervised joy ride. Along the way, students will learn about pony care, nutrition, grooming, health, and daily stable operations in a safe, engaging, and educational environment.

Suitable for schools, preschools, childcare centres, and educational groups. Joy rides are available for children aged 3 years and above.
