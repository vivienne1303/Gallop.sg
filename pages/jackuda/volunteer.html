Volunteer Programme

Volunteer with Us

As a volunteer, you will help the stable in many ways such as stable management, including grooming and showering, tacking, exercise, events and many more. In the process, you will also learn and get first hand insight on what is involved in caring for our horses and running the stables.

Gallop Stable's volunteer program offers more opportunities for our riders to spend time and learn more about their four-legged friends. Please note that we require volunteers to commit at least 1/2 day a week for at least 3 months. Experience with horses is preferred.

For more information about our program, please email us at enquiry@gallopstable.com.
