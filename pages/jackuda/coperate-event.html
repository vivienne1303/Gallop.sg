Group activities for every occasion

Gallop Jackuda welcomes corporate outings, team-building events, family days, schools, youth and church groups, CCA groups and anyone looking for a fun day with horses.

From small celebrations to large family functions, every programme can be customised around your group size, venue, schedule and preferred activities.

Public pony rides, birthday pony rides, carriage rides and selected outside-event experiences can also be arranged upon request.
