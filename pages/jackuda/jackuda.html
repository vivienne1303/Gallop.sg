Jackuda Activities

Pony Rides, Trail Rides & Pony Feeding

Enjoy a fun-filled day with pony rides, trail rides, and pony feeding experiences. These hands-on activities offer children and adults the opportunity to interact with our friendly four-legged companions, creating meaningful connections and lasting memories.

Art & Craft

Get creative at our art and craft corner. It is a fun way to unwind, explore your creativity, and spend quality time together, making it a meaningful family bonding activity for visitors of all ages.

Camps and Workshops

Join our interactive camps and workshops for a fun and immersive introduction to the world of horses. Participants can learn essential horsemanship skills, gain insights into horse care and handling, and experience life around the stables through engaging, hands-on activities. These sessions provide a unique opportunity to learn, connect with horses and ponies, and build confidence through meaningful experiences with our equine companions.

Events

Celebrate special occasions with our beloved horses and ponies. Host birthday parties, wedding photoshoots, corporate team-building sessions, and private gatherings at our stables, or bring the experience outdoors with our pony and horse hire services for a truly memorable event.
