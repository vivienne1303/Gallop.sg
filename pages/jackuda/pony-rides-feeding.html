Family-friendly pony experiences

Children aged 3 years and above can take a pony ride on our lovely ponies. These rides are a great way for kids to experience the thrills of horse-back riding in a safe environment. Horse rides for adults are also available at our Pasir Ris Park Stables. Helmets will be provided and our rides are fully supervised and led by our trained staff.
