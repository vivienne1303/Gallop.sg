Outdoor riding

A memorable journey on horseback

Available for all riding levels, our trail rides welcome both experienced and first-time riders looking for a short getaway. Enjoy the lush greenery and nature of Bukit Timah along an off-road track through a unique and lesser-seen side of Singapore.

You may encounter local wildlife along the way, including monitor lizards, monkeys and jungle fowl. Every session is arranged according to rider experience, weather, horse suitability and availability.

We can also customise personalised trail packages for special occasions such as Valentine's Day, birthdays, proposals, anniversaries and more.
