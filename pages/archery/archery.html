An adventurous way to connect with horses

Gallop Horseback Archery brings together the ancient art of archery and the timeless bond with horses right here in Singapore. Open to beginners and seasoned riders alike, our programs invite you to learn the skill of focus, balance, and trust as you ride and aim in harmony with your horse. Choose from our ground archery, trail archery, or step up to full horseback archery. Each session sharpens your senses, builds confidence, and invites you into a new, adventurous way of connecting with your horse.
