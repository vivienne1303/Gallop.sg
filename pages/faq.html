Before you ride

Plan your visit with confidence

Requirements vary by lesson type and rider suitability. These answers cover the most common questions from new riders and families.

How long are pony rides?

Pony rides at Pasir Ris Park and Admiralty take approximately 3 to 5 minutes per round. Pony rides at the Downtown East stable take approximately 2 to 3 minutes per round.

What attire should I wear?

The recommended attire is long pants and covered shoes.

What is the weight limit for pony and horse rides?

The weight limit for pony rides is 40 kg. The weight limit for horse rides is 75 kg.

How do we book riding lessons?

You can book lessons through WhatsApp or email us at enquiry@gallopstable.com. Advance booking of at least 2 to 3 days is required.

Do we need to book in advance for pony rides?

No advance booking is required, as pony rides are usually available during operating hours. However, we recommend calling before you visit because rides may be unavailable due to bad weather or private bookings.

Still unsure which experience is suitable? Contact our team with the rider's age, height, weight, and riding experience.
