Before you ride

Plan your visit with confidence

Requirements vary by lesson type and rider suitability. These answers cover the most common questions from new riders and families.

What attire should I wear while riding a horse?

Wear comfortable fitted clothing, long pants or breeches, and covered shoes with a small heel. A securely fitted riding helmet is compulsory. Avoid loose clothing, slippers, sandals, dresses, and accessories that may catch on equipment.

Helmets and riding boots are available for rental at S$5 each. Lesson students may also be required to wear the Gallop T-shirt.

Is there an age limit to ride a pony or horse?

Age requirements depend on the program. Pony introductory lessons are available for children aged 3, while junior pony lessons are designed for ages 4 to 12. Private horse lessons generally start from age 6, and semi-private or group horse lessons start from age 7.

Walk-in rides and other experiences may have different requirements. The stable team will confirm the most suitable option for each rider.

Is there a weight limit to ride a horse?

Yes. The current limit for horse riding lessons is 75 kg and below. Pony lesson programs have a limit of 40 kg and below. These limits help protect the wellbeing of both horse and rider.

Final participation depends on the rider's height, balance, experience, and the suitable horses available.

Still unsure which experience is suitable? Contact our team with the rider's age, height, weight, and riding experience.
