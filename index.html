About Gallop Stable

Accessible horse riding since 2003

Gallop Stable began at Pasir Ris Park with 13 ponies. Our mission is to keep horse riding affordable, welcoming and memorable for every family in Singapore.

  • Started in 2003 at Pasir Ris Park
  • Stables at Horsecity/Bukit Timah and Pasir Ris/Downtown East
  • Friendly instructors and premium stable facilities
